## Non-negotiable rules

- Use only vector-style visuals; never generate realistic people.
- Do not let Canva redraw or modify the KAILA logo, mascot, or QR codes.
- Keep all essential copy inside the central safe area.
- Registration is the primary action; Android download is secondary.
- Do not promise guaranteed jobs, earnings, or approval.
- Use KAILA Blue `#1463FF`, Cyan `#27B7FF`, Ink `#0A1220`, and white.
- Use Inter or the closest clean rounded sans-serif available.
