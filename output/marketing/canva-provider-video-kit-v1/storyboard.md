## Text hierarchy

- Headlines: uppercase, heavy, maximum two lines.
- Supporting copy: short enough to read in under two seconds.
- CTA frame: registration QR larger or equally prominent; download QR remains secondary.
- Never place text below the Reels bottom-interface safe area.

## Transition notes

- Use the route line as the recurring transition device.
- Let tool icons travel along the route between scenes.
- Use one cyan sweep for the Android scene.
- Stop all decorative motion during the final QR frame.
