# KAILA Skill Drop

## Campaign idea

Recruit providers through a small act of proof: upload one piece of work they are proud of. The campaign leads with identity and craftsmanship, not a generic job opportunity.

## Primary message

“Isang gawa. Isang kuwento. Ipakita mo.”

## Conversion path

1. Register as a KAILA Service Provider.
2. Add a service.
3. Upload one best-work photo to begin a portfolio.

## Why it is different

- It turns recruitment into a participatory social challenge.
- It gives providers an immediate first action instead of a list of platform benefits.
- It creates a natural comment prompt and opportunities for future provider spotlights.
- It demonstrates KAILA’s portfolio value through the campaign mechanic itself.

## Suggested rollout

- Launch post: the supplied key visual and caption.
- Follow-up carousel: “The work / The story / The provider.”
- Community prompt: ask providers to name the first project they would showcase.
- Retargeting post: remind interested providers that one strong photo is enough to begin.

Do not promise guaranteed jobs, earnings, ranking, verification, or featuring.
