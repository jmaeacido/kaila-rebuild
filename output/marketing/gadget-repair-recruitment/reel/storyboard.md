# KAILA Gadget Repair Recruitment Reel

Format: Facebook / Instagram Reel (`1080 × 1920`, `9:16`), ~26 seconds, 30 fps.

## Storyboard

| Time | Visual | On-screen text | Voiceover (Bisaya) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | Bull with phone + repair tools; gadget icons | **GAMAY RA ANG NAKAILA?** / Sa imong repair skills… | “Hoy! Isa ka ba ka-owner o technician sa cellphone ug gadget repair?” |
| 0:05–0:11 | Shop + home service icons; sparse discovery | **SHOP O HOME SERVICE** / Pipila ra ang nakaila nimo. | “O modawat ka ba og home service, pero pipila ra ang nakaila nimo?” |
| 0:11–0:17 | Bull thumbs-up; connection lines to local requests | **PWEDE KA SA KAILA!** / Mas makita ang imong skills. | “Ayos! Pwede ka diri sa KAILA! Mas makita ang imong skills ug serbisyo!” |
| 0:17–0:26 | Static CTA: Bull + official wordmark + branded registration QR | **APIL ISIP SERVICE PROVIDER** / **PAKITA SA KAILA.** | “Apil isip Service Provider! E-scan ang QR code. Pakita sa KAILA!” |

## Non-negotiables

- Vector-only visuals; no realistic people.
- Official KAILA wordmark + Bull mascot on every frame.
- Final frame uses the official KAILA-branded registration QR (not AI-generated modules).
- Hold CTA/QR for at least 4 seconds with no decorative motion.
- Do not promise guaranteed jobs, earnings, or approval.
